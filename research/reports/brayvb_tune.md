## 4. Verdict

Best nested-OOS procedure: 1d/real (Sharpe 0.66, DSR p 0.029). Largest gain over default: 1h/real (-0.11 vs 0.13).

**Tuning does not help.** Nested walk-forward OOS Sharpe of "tune on the training window, trade the next 6 months" is below the untuned default preset on every tf x source (1h -0.11/-0.14 vs 0.13/0.41; 4h 0.07/-0.06 vs 0.75/0.87; 1d 0.66/0.13 vs 1.15/0.95), and the tuned winner beats the default in only 1-3 of 6 folds. Train Sharpes of 1.5-2.9 collapse to ~0 or negative on test: classic overfitting of a 13-dimension space (~2,000 configs per group). Per-fold winners disagree (poles, period, mult all jump around; only weak tendencies like mode_lag=False on 1h, os low on 4h/1d), so there is no stable optimum to find.

Deflated Sharpe p stays at or near 0 for every tuned procedure (best 0.029, 1d real) and for the default (best 0.18, 1d real), with N a lower bound. The final all-IS configs look good in-sample (IS Sharpe 0.5-1.65; the OOS-window columns are contaminated) and the 4h/1d ones sit on plateaus (neighbour mean close to optimum, min 0.5-0.8), but their PBO is 0.47-0.80 on 4h/1d (gate is <0.2); only 1h real (PBO 0.14) passes PBO, and it has a negative nested OOS Sharpe.

**Recommendation:** do not adopt any tuned config.
